# How Helena Lives — care, translated into technology

> For my sister living with schizophrenia: a record of her days — Scene · Feel · Way · Next —
> and beside it, a 24-hour guardian (the care daemon).

> Part of the [**Helena Content Foundry**](https://github.com/helena751107/helena_phone) — mobile-first, MCP-verified.

---

## What this is

The story of my sister's life. Not a medical chart, not an administrative record.
An honest record of **how she made it through today**, held within faith · music · family care.

It runs in two lanes:

| Lane | Folder | What |
|------|--------|------|
| **Life story** | `docs/tracks/` · `docs/dialogue/` | Chapters of a life and the record of a day (Scene / Feel / Way / Next) |
| **Care daemon (guardian)** | `docs/care-daemon/` | The tech that keeps watch — battery · temperature · location · remote care |

## Guardrails

- **Never commit my sister's personal identifiers, medical records, accounts, or SSN.** (No name · location · photo.)
- **No legal or medical assertion** — defer to "needs confirmation".
- The publishing engine lives in `helena_phone`; here there is only the design map (`docs/solutions/`) and CI.
